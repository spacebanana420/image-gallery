## Technical details

My artworks uploaded to this repository are converted 4 times:
* Small WEBP version for previews in this page
* Medium-size WEBP version for previews in each gallery
* High-resolution WEBP version for previews in collection pages
* High-reoslution JPG version

The original images are in PNG, usually at 2560x1600 or 2560x1440 resolution, but here they are converted to 1920 pixels wide JPG to spare on file size.
